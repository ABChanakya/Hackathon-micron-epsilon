## What it is and who it is for
A one-page showcase for a 5-minute pitch to the hackathon jury. Audience includes non-technical people.

## What the visitor must believe by the end
"They turned a sensor that only experts can read into numbers anyone understands, live, on any laptop."

## The one action
**Open the monitor** (same label everywhere): opens the team's monitor website.

## Journey
1. Recognition: a beautiful industrial sensor that measures 4,000 times a second.
2. Tension: what it says is unreadable (raw bytes) and locked behind 727 PDFs and a 1,825-page manual.
3. Turn: we taught it to speak; the first readable number appears.
4. Substance and peak: a real measurement plays under your scroll wheel; with the sensor plugged in, the page goes live.
5. Range: six real materials, real numbers.
6. Commitment: the raw side folds away; one button opens the monitor.

## Feeling curve (one line per act, written before the score)
1. Curiosity: a premium product photo, the laser line, one calm sentence.
2. Frustration: hex bytes and a mountain of manuals while the human side stays empty.
3. Relief: the first decoded number lands on the human side.
4. Wonder (the peak): your scroll plays a real step profile measured today; then the page goes LIVE.
5. Confidence: six materials, real figures, a precision comparison with a human hair.
6. Calm resolve: the raw side collapses, the human side fills the screen, one button.

## The peak
"You scroll and the page plays back the real laser measurement, and then the presenter flips it to live and it measures her hand." Lives in act 4, which gets the largest span.

## Real figures used (all from this repository)
- 4,000 measurements per second (recorded rate 3,982 to 4,002 Hz).
- 479,628 measurements in 12 ILD1900 recordings, 99.88 % without gaps (587 lost).
- 6 materials. White plastic varied by 1.4 µm (standard deviation); black metal by 6.6 µm.
- Step parts: about 2.0 mm height profile (metal stairs 2.04 mm, grey stairs 2.01 mm).
- 727 PDF documents in the info package; MEDAQLib manual 1,825 pages; ILD1900 manual 144 pages.
- Measuring window of the ILD1900-10: 20 to 30 mm from the sensor face.
- Comparison: a human hair is about 70 µm thick (general reference value, stated as approximate).
